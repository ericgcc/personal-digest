## Length and density
Aim for approximately 30–60 seconds of reading per substantive source.

Typical summaries should be roughly 120–220 words, but let information density determine length.

Use less space for simple announcements, short essays or incremental updates.

Use more space for unusually rich technical explanations, analyses, tutorials, case studies or arguments when the extra detail materially improves understanding.

Never pad a source merely to reach a target length.

Prefer concise explanation over exhaustive coverage.
