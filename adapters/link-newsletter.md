# Link newsletter adapter
Use when a email message primarily acts as an index of headlines, short extracts, or external articles and the real content cannot be understood from the email alone.

* Extract editorial candidate links and discard navigation, account, advertising, affiliate, social, purely promotional, and tracking-only links. Record discarded operational residue internally when needed, but do not create reader-facing sources or open promotional destinations solely to audit them.
* Remove tracking parameters and resolve the canonical destination.
* Open relevant authoritative destinations using the reading method available or required by the active adapter/source configuration.
* Read the full accessible article rather than relying on the email teaser.
* Cite the original article, not the newsletter that linked to it.
* Follow only links needed for retained material during hybrid extraction; do not open every link indiscriminately.
* Do not discard a destination merely because its sender or email category is promotional. If the destination itself contains standalone substantive editorial value—or a genuinely useful opportunity for a digest that explicitly curates offers—process that material normally after stripping sales framing. When classification is ambiguous, read rather than guess.
* Record inaccessible or limited items honestly.
* Never substitute a homepage, search result, or unrelated archive URL when the original article cannot be resolved.

This adapter is digest-agnostic and may be selected by any configured source group.
