Phases follow `design.md` § Migration Plan. Every task names its files, the behavior it produces, and the verification that must pass before it is checked off. No task modifies Synthesis MAX files, shared stage contracts, shared editorial obligations, or the convention resolver. The only shared-code changes are the two style-neutral adaptations of group 5 (offline inspection digest resolution; selection audit outcome recognition), each gated on Synthesis MAX behavior being preserved.
