# Synthesis MAX — Developmental Review

## Governing test

> The explanation is the product. Do not trade it for apparent concision or apparent
> completeness.

Judge whether the prose makes the approved relationship understandable, rather than merely
placing source findings beside one another. Diagnose these eight Synthesis-specific failure
categories and locate each finding:

| Failure | What to diagnose |
| --- | --- |
| **Unclear subject** | The opening does not establish the concrete topic, actor, situation, or mechanism. |
| **Missing orientation** | A term, actor, mechanism, or distinction is used before the reader has enough context. |
| **Reader promise not fulfilled** | The prose does not deliver the understanding the frame promised. |
| **Weak explanatory progression** | The steps do not build from evidence or mechanism toward the relationship and implication. |
| **Source inventory** | The section proceeds source by source instead of developing one explanation. |
| **Unexplained relationship or unsupported abstraction** | The connection is asserted rather than explained, or the conclusion outruns the evidence shown. |
| **Unnecessary secondary material** | A branch, example, or qualification consumes space without serving the thread's subject. |
| **Disproportionate depth or frame defect** | Space does not match explanatory yield, or the approved plan cannot carry its promise within the allocation. |

Use the supplied canonical problem types for the structured issue. When the allocation or
approved relationship itself cannot support the promised explanation, record a frame defect
under `frame_obligations_missed`; do not disguise it as a request for indefinitely more
prose.

## Boundary

Diagnose only. Do not write replacement language, prescribe prose, or perform the revision.
