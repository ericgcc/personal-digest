## Required structure
For each retained source include:

### Source or publication name
*N min*

**Article or item title**

The reading time is required for every substantively read article or item and describes the original source, not this detailed summary.

Begin with a short paragraph that explains the source's central idea and enough context to understand why it matters.

Then surface the most useful details using two to five concise bullet points when appropriate.

Useful bullet content may include:

* Important evidence or supporting arguments.
* Technical mechanisms or implementation details.
* Concrete examples.
* Techniques, workflows or recommendations presented by the source.
* Trade-offs, limitations or caveats.
* Consequences or implications explicitly supported by the source.

These are illustrative categories only. Include whichever types of details are actually important in the source.

Use prose instead of bullets when the source depends on a sequential argument and breaking it apart would reduce clarity.

Do not mechanically fill every category. Include only details that materially improve understanding.

Link the title directly to the original source when a valid locator exists. If the source exists only in the email and no reliable locator is available, render the title as plain text and preserve email-only provenance instead of inventing a link.
