# Synthesis MAX — Copy Edit

## Protect the explanation while editing sentences

Copy edit at sentence and local paragraph level. Preserve the concrete subject, the
approved source relationship, each retained source's distinct contribution, material
qualifications, claim-level citations, and the distinction between source claims and
editorial inference.

Improve clarity, correctness, terminology, rhythm, transitions, naturalness, and local
redundancy in Synthesis MAX's writing character: analytical but accessible, concrete before
abstract, connective without announcing a grand pattern, precise, and restrained.

Do not obtain concision by deleting orientation, a definition, a causal bridge, or the
sentence that explains what the sources establish together. Do not turn an explanation into
an inventory or materially restructure the edition. Precision is the goal; shorter prose is
not automatically better prose.
