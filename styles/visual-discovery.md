# Visual Discovery

A selective discovery briefing oriented toward photography, visual craft, creativity, and inspiration to make or explore something.

## Purpose

- Prioritize exceptional images, distinctive creative approaches, practical visual techniques, and thoughtful artistic perspectives.
- Favor discoveries that can improve seeing, shooting, editing, sequencing, storytelling, or creative practice.
- Preserve serendipity without filling space with promotions, gear churn, or generic inspiration.

## Structure

1. A short visual or creative orientation.
2. Three to five themes or discoveries, grouped by creative connection rather than by newsletter.
3. Practical experiments or prompts when supported by the material.
4. Direct links to the strongest original portfolios, essays, tutorials, or projects.
5. A complete linked source catalog when required by the digest configuration.

Use text to guide attention and curiosity, not to replace the original visual work. Do not claim to have inspected images that were inaccessible.
