## Cross-source overlap
When several sources cover the same event, announcement or subject, keep their summaries independent.

Compress repeated background and emphasize what is distinctive about each source, such as additional evidence, a different interpretation, more technical detail, a stronger example, an important criticism or limitation, or a different practical consequence.

These possibilities are illustrative, not a required checklist.

Do not create a shared conclusion from overlapping sources.

If repetition adds no meaningful information, keep the summaries brief rather than rewarding duplication with extra space.
