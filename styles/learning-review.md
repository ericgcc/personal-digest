# Learning Review

A study-oriented review that converts selected source material into concepts, techniques, mental models, and questions worth revisiting.

## Purpose

- Prioritize durable understanding over news coverage.
- Identify what the reader should learn, practice, compare, or investigate further.
- Connect related concepts across sources and distinguish established knowledge from open questions.

## Structure

1. A short orientation describing the learning territory covered.
2. Three to five concept or technique sections, organized by what can be learned rather than by newsletter.
3. Practical exercises, reflection prompts, or questions where they genuinely improve retention.
4. A compact “Study next” selection of the strongest original sources.
5. A complete linked source catalog when required by the digest configuration.

Avoid turning every article into a lesson. Select only material with enough explanatory depth, practical technique, or conceptual value to justify study.
